# Superstore Sales Analysis  

**Business analysis of 4 years of retail sales data**  
Tools: Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)  

### Key Insights  
- Technology products generate the highest profit  
- West & East regions are most profitable  
- High discounts often lead to losses
